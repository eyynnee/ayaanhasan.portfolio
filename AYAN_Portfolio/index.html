Project Scope & Methodology

A deterministic tsunami risk assessment of Karachi's coastal belt evaluating shoreline inundation dynamics. The project integrated bathymetric and topographic elevation data into a continuous Digital Elevation Model (DEM), validated against field GNSS ground control points, and executed GeoClaw shallow water numerical tsunami simulations across critical waterfront infrastructure developments.

Project Outputs (Team Simulation)
  • GeoClaw numerical hydrodynamic tsunami simulations
  • Wave arrival times, wave heights, and maximum inundation extents
  • Building typology physical vulnerability assessment
  • Spatial risk maps and coastal mitigation recommendations

Technical Scope & Contribution

DEM Development & GIS Workflow (Independent Execution)

Independent execution of the complete DEM and GIS workflow:

  • Researched DEM generation methodologies for coastal transition zones.
  • Selected, harmonized, and prepared elevation datasets (GEBCO bathymetry and SRTM topography).
  • Planned and executed GNSS field survey work to collect high precision Ground Control Points (GCPs).
  • Developed the seamless integrated bathymetric topographic DEM in QGIS.
  • Statistically validated the DEM against field GCPs, achieving an RMSE of ±2.98 m.
  • Produced cartographic spatial risk maps, elevation profiles, and technical documentation.
  • Coordinated the elevation data model integration with numerical tsunami simulations.
